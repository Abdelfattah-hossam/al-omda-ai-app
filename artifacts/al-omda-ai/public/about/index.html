About AL OMDA Ai

AL OMDA Ai is an Arabic-first AI workspace for chat, document creation and project planning. Use Arabic or English to prepare Word documents, Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint presentations and HTML files. Review the project plan and its steps before execution.

Use the app in a web browser or see the preview downloads for Android, Windows and Mac. On iPhone and iPad, add the website to your Home Screen in Safari. There is currently no App Store release. AI tools require internet access. An account is needed to save and sync work. Check important facts and generated files before using them.
